### Topic: Roblox selling and payout rules

- Date researched: `2026-10-16`
- Question: How can a beginner with no budget get paid from Roblox work?
- Sources:
  - https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/sell-on-creator-store — USD selling rules — 2026-10-16
  - https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange — DevEx rules — 2026-10-16
- Facts:
  - Only Models ($2.99–$49.99) and Plugins ($4.99–$249.99) can be priced in USD; group-owned assets are excluded.
  - Sellers must be 18+, or 13–17 with a parent's consent, use 2-Step Verification, pass age/ID verification, and live in a Stripe-supported country (not e.g. Brazil, China, India, Russia).
  - Sellers keep net proceeds after taxes and payment fees; sales sit 30 days in escrow and pay monthly.
  - DevEx needs age 13+ and 30,000 earned Robux; standard rate 0.0038 USD per Robux (30,000 Robux ≈ $114).
- Estimates: none yet.
- Inferences:
  - Creator Store models reach buyers without an audience, which fits a user with no community.
  - DevEx is a slow route: a new game must earn 30,000 Robux before any cash-out.
- Unknowns: which model types sell, typical sales volume, how crowded each category is.
- Customer evidence: none yet.
- Competitors and substitutes: free Creator Store models, CC0 packs (Kenney, Poly Haven).
- Price evidence: only Roblox's price limits; no sales data.
- Recommended test: publish 3 free models, measure installs/favourites for 14 days, then one paid pack.
- Confidence: high for the rules, low for demand.
