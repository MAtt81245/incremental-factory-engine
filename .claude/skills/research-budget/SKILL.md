# Research Budget

## Rules

- Do not browse unless the answer depends on current information.
- Use a small number of high-quality sources.
- Stop when enough evidence exists to answer the question.
- Do not keep re-researching the same question.
- Use the repository research files before repeating work.

## Output habit

When doing research, clearly separate:

- Facts
- Estimates
- Inferences
- Opinions
- Unknowns

## For business and money research

- Check whether there is evidence of customer demand.
- Check whether people already pay for similar products.
- Check for substitutes and competitors.
- Check startup cost and time to first revenue.
- Do not claim the idea is profitable without evidence.

## Stop condition

Stop when you have enough to answer the question well and you know the next step.
